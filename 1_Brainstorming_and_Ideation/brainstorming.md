\# 1. Brainstorming and Ideation



\## Project Name



EduGenie – AI-Powered Personalized Learning Assistant



\## Problem Statement



Students often have different learning levels and different ways of understanding a topic. A single explanation may not be suitable for a school student, college student, or advanced learner.



Students also need support for learning, question answering, summarization, quizzes, and personalized learning paths.



EduGenie is designed to provide these learning features through an AI-powered educational assistant.



\## Proposed Solution



EduGenie is an AI-powered learning assistant that helps students understand educational topics through:



\- Topic explanations

\- Question answering

\- Text summarization

\- Quiz generation

\- Personalized learning paths



The explanation feature supports different learning levels such as School, College, and Advanced.



\## Target Users



\- School students

\- College students

\- Advanced learners

\- Students preparing for examinations

\- Learners who need personalized explanations



\## Key Features



\### 1. AI Explanation



Explains a topic according to the selected learning level.



\### 2. Question and Answer



Provides answers to questions asked by the learner.



\### 3. Summary



Generates concise summaries from provided content.



\### 4. Quiz Generation



Creates multiple-choice questions for learning and self-assessment.



\### 5. Personalized Learning Path



Creates a structured learning plan for a selected topic.



\## Expected Outcome



The goal of EduGenie is to provide students with a simple AI-based learning assistant that can adapt explanations and provide multiple learning-support features in one application.
